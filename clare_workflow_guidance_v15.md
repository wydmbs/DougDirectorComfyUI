WEIGH-UP vs FLF2V: they are complementary, NOT competitors. NEGATIVE PROMPT COMPOSITION |
How to combine | For every shot: MASTER NEGATIVE PROMPT (this sheet) + that shot's negative_prompt_ADD (Shot List, column M). The per-shot ADD column covers three things the master can't: (1) tonal bleed — the opposite mood of what that beat needs, so calm shots don't drift dramatic and storm shots don't drift placid, (2) pig/rooster cross-contamination — only added on shots where a character actually appears, since the two LoRAs are separately trained and risk bleeding into each other, (3) the human rule — full human face/figure negated on every shot except 11.5, where it's relaxed only enough to allow the obscured-by-hat hammock guy to exist.
Why per-shot instead of one master list | A single master negative can only defend the lowest common denominator (rendering quality). It can't tell a calm shot from a storm shot, so it can't push back when the model defaults toward the wrong mood for that specific beat. Per-shot negatives let the storm beats actively suppress 'calm/static' and the peaceful-sinking beats actively suppress 'chaotic/violent' — which matters most exactly at the two places this storyboard relies on tonal contrast (Beat 4 to Beat 5, and Beat 9 to Beat 10).
